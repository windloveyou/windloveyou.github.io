关于我About Me

你好!我是黄天择,即将进入华中师范大学攻读计算机技术专业硕士学位,本科就读于苏州工学院物联网工程专业(嵌入式培养)。

我的研究兴趣集中在计算机视觉,尤其是注意力机制与模型轻量化(剪枝、量化与边缘端部署)。 我以第一作者身份提出了高效空间敏感部分通道注意力机制 ESPCA,并在 AICCC 2024 发表; 也有从算法设计、TensorRT / INT8 优化到 Jetson Nano、树莓派等边缘设备落地的完整项目经验。 我相信让模型"又好又快地跑在真实设备上"与提出新方法同样重要。

⭐ 我的目标:成为一名优秀的人工智能算法工程师。

Hello! I am Tianze Huang, an incoming master's student in Computer Technology at Central China Normal University. I received my B.Eng. in IoT Engineering (Embedded Systems Track) from Suzhou University of Technology.

My research interests center on computer vision, especially attention mechanisms and model compression (pruning, quantization, and edge deployment). I proposed ESPCA, an efficient spatial-sensitive partial channel attention mechanism, published as a first-author paper at AICCC 2024. I also have end-to-end experience taking models from algorithm design through TensorRT / INT8 optimization to deployment on edge devices such as Jetson Nano and Raspberry Pi. I believe making models run fast and well on real devices matters as much as inventing new methods.

⭐ My goal: to become an excellent AI algorithm engineer.
