发表论文Publications

2024
ESPCA: An Efficient Spatial-Sensitive Partial Channel Attention Mechanism in CNNs
Tianze Huang (第一作者) (First Author)
针对经典注意力机制(SE、CBAM、CA)推理延迟高、通道特征冗余的问题,提出在部分通道上结合 CA 注意力的 ESPCA 机制, 并采用非降维方法保持通道与权重的直接对应关系;在 ImageNet、MS COCO 等数据集上验证了其对目标检测与图像分类精度的提升。
ESPCA addresses the high inference latency and channel redundancy of classic attention mechanisms (SE, CBAM, CA) by applying CA attention to partial channels with a non-dimensionality-reduction design, improving both object detection and image classification accuracy on ImageNet and MS COCO.
